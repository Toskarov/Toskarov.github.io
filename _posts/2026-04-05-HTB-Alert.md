Let's begin this new machine *(Alert - 10.129.231.119)* scanning the ports with NMAP

`sudo nmap -p- --open --min-rate 5000 -sS -vvv -n -Pn 10.129.231.119 -oG allPorts`

***Breackdown of the command***

- `-p-` all ports

- `--open` Show only open ports

- `-sS` stealthy scan. Instead of sending a three-way handshake, when a response is received, a SYN is sent > a SYN/ACK is received, and the process ends with an RST.
This allows you to quickly determine whether the service is active.

- `--min-rate 5000` with this flag We do not send packets at a rate lower than 5,000 per second.

- `-vvv` With this flag, when ports are detected, they are displayed immediately, rather than waiting for the scan to finish.

- `-n` We don't want DNS resolution to apply to us

- `-Pn` We don't want host discovery (ARP) to be applied to us

- `-oG` output grep because we want output in grep format
